Spark Write Test: temporary buffer

mySpark's first write. Loads a preset into the amp's temporary buffer (0x7f), switches to it, then reads the live sound back and compares.

What this changes: only the sound the amp is playing right now. No saved slot is written. Pressing PRESET on the amp brings back your saved sound.
Safety: you can only load presets this page has just read from your own amp, so every model in them is one the amp has. Writes are refused unless the amp is "Spark LIVE BLE".
